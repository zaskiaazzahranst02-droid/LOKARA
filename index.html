LOKARA is your travel companion to discover the beauty of Yogyakarta. From historical landmarks and breathtaking landscapes to traditional culture and local experiences, we help you explore the city in a meaningful way.

Popular Destinations

Discover the iconic places and hidden beauty of Yogyakarta.

HISTORICAL LANDMARK

Borobudur Temple

Explore the magnificent Buddhist temple, famous for its ancient architecture and historical reliefs.

HISTORICAL SITE

Taman Sari

Discover the former royal garden and bathing complex of the Yogyakarta Sultanate.

SHOPPING & CULTURE

Malioboro Street

Enjoy local shops, traditional street food, souvenirs, and the lively atmosphere of Yogyakarta.

HISTORICAL LANDMARK

Prambanan Temple

Admire the impressive Hindu temple complex and its beautiful ancient architecture.

NATURE & BEACH

Parangtritis Beach

Enjoy the beautiful coastline, ocean breeze, and famous sunset views in Bantul.

NATURE ESCAPE

Mangunan Pine Forest

Relax among tall pine trees and enjoy the peaceful natural atmosphere of Mangunan.

HISTORICAL SITE

Ratu Boko Palace

Explore the remains of an ancient palace and enjoy beautiful views of the surrounding landscape.

ADVENTURE

Goa Pindul

Discover the underground river and unique cave scenery in Gunungkidul.

LOCAL HERITAGE

Keraton Yogyakarta

Discover Javanese royal traditions, architecture, and cultural heritage.

SCENIC LANDSCAPE

Tebing Breksi

See unique limestone formations and enjoy the beautiful landscape of this former mining site.

FAMOUS BEACH

Pantai Indrayanti

Enjoy the beautiful white sand, clear water, and coastal scenery of Gunungkidul.
